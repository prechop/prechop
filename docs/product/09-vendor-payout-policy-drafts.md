# Vendor payout policy drafts

These are versioned internal drafts only. They are not published terms and must not be presented as legal escrow language.

## Draft v2.0 — pending external confirmation

- Payment success, order completion and vendor payout are separate states.
- A successful payment does not make the vendor immediately payable.
- Trusted completion requires QR/PIN evidence or an audited support confirmation.
- An eligible, undisputed order enters the next business-day 10:00 Africa/Lagos run after a 24-hour grace period.
- Balances below ₦1,000 roll forward indefinitely.
- Prechop initially absorbs transfer fees and qualifying stamp duty; final treatment remains externally pending.
- Order issues create an order-specific hold. Normal vendor suspension does not confiscate valid completed earnings.
- Post-payout refunds create an auditable vendor debt/adjustment.
- Processing depends on Paystack balance availability and banking systems and is not a guaranteed T+1 bank credit.

Pending: reserve policy, marketplace/custody treatment, contracts, tax, chargeback liability, safeguarding and regulatory/accounting requirements.
